# YouTube Shorts Scripts (Hinglish) - Viral Pack #1

Har Short 30-45 second ka hai. Rule: pehle 2 second mein hook, aakhir mein loop ya sawaal, taaki log comment karein aur dobara dekhein.

---

## Short 1: THE HAUNTED SCHOOL Teaser (horror, 30 sec)

**Title:** Raat ke 12 baje purane school mein gaye... 😱 #Shorts
**Hook (0-2 sec):** Kaali screen. Dheemi awaaz: "Raat ke 12 baje... school ka gate khud khul gaya."
**Visual:** Aarav, Kabir aur Meera torch lekar gate par. Piche toota hua school.

**Voiceover:**
> Teen dost. Ek purana school. Aur raat ke 12 baje.
> Kabir bola, "Bhai, wapas chalte hain!"
> Par Aarav ko ek diary mili... jo kisi bachche ki nahi thi.
> Diary ke aakhri page par likha tha: "Teacher abhi bhi padha rahi hain."
> Aur tabhi... peeche se kisi ke kadmon ki aawaaz aayi.

**On-screen text:** "3 dost. 1 raat. 1 class jo kabhi khatam nahi hui."
**Aakhri 2 sec (loop):** Khidki mein ek bachcha: "Excuse me... next class shuru ho rahi hai?" -> kaali screen.
**CTA:** "Poori film channel par hai. Tum raat ko akele us school mein jaate? Comment karo."

## Short 2: Library Ki Kitaab (horror, 35 sec)

**Title:** Is kitaab mein tumhara aaj ka din likha tha... 😨 #Shorts
**Hook:** "School ki library mein ek kitaab hai, jise koi issue nahi karta."

**Voiceover:**
> Rohan ne library ki aakhri shelf se ek purani kitaab uthayi.
> Cover par kuch nahi likha tha. Pehle page par bas ek naam tha: Rohan.
> Usne socha, koi mazaak hai. Par har page par uska aaj ka din likha tha. Minute by minute.
> "4:10 - tum library mein aaye."
> "4:12 - tumne yeh kitaab uthayi."
> Aakhri page par sirf ek line thi: "4:15 - peeche mudkar mat dekhna."
> Library ki ghadi mein 4:14 baj chuke the.

**Ending:** Ghadi ki tik-tik, phir achanak kaali screen.
**CTA:** "Agar tum Rohan hote, peeche dekhte? 'Haan' ya 'Nahi' likho."

## Short 3: Papa Ki Chappal (emotional, 45 sec)

**Title:** Papa ne kabhi nahi bataya... 🥺 #Shorts
**Hook:** "Papa ki chappal ek saal se toot rahi thi, par meri fees kabhi nahi ruki."

**Voiceover:**
> Mere papa ne kabhi nahi bataya ki unke paas paise kam hain.
> Har mahine meri fees time par bharte the.
> Ek din maine dekha, unki chappal ka strap safety pin se juda tha.
> Maine poocha, "Papa, nayi kyun nahi li?"
> Woh haste hue bole, "Abhi yeh chal rahi hai beta. Tu bas padh."
> Aaj meri pehli salary aayi. Sabse pehle maine unke liye chappal nahi, poore joote kharide.
> Papa bole, "Itna mehnga kyun laya?" Aur unki aankhon mein paani tha.

**On-screen text (aakhir mein):** "Aaj apne papa ko ek call zaroor karo. ❤️"
**CTA:** "Apne papa ka naam comment mein likho."

## Short 4: Aakhri Bench (horror twist, 40 sec)

**Title:** Class ki aakhri bench par koi nahi baithta, kyun? 👻 #Shorts
**Hook:** "Class ki sabse peeche wali bench hamesha khaali rehti thi."

**Voiceover:**
> Naye student Veer ne wahi bench chuni. Sab ne roka, usne nahi suna.
> Pehle din uski copy mein kisi ne likha: "Yahan se hat jao."
> Doosre din: "Yeh meri jagah hai."
> Teesre din Veer ne bench ke neeche dekha. Ek purana ID card. Naam: Veer. Class: 10-B. Saal: 1994.
> Wahi naam. Wahi photo.
> Teacher ne attendance li, "Veer?" "Yes sir."
> Teacher ne kaha, "Tum aaj bhi aa gaye? Tumhe gaye toh tees saal ho gaye."

**Ending:** Khaali bench par ID card hilta hua, kaali screen.
**CTA:** "Tumhare school ki sabse darawani jagah kaunsi thi? Comment karo."

## Short 5: Maa Ki Ek Line (motivation, 40 sec)

**Title:** Fail hone par Maa ne yeh kaha... 😭 #Shorts
**Hook:** "Jis din main 12th mein fail hua, Maa ne daantne ki jagah thali parosi."

**Voiceover:**
> Main darwaze par result haath mein liye khada tha. Socha, aaj toh pitai pakki hai.
> Maa ne bas itna kaha, "Pehle khana kha le. Fail hua hai, khatam nahi hua."
> Us raat maine pehli baar mehsoos kiya ki girne ke baad bhi koi saath khada ho sakta hai.
> Agle saal maine dobara koshish ki. Is baar pass hua.
> Maa ki woh ek line aaj bhi mere saath chalti hai.

**On-screen text (aakhir mein):** "Fail hone wale ko daant nahi, saath chahiye."
**CTA:** "Yeh un sabko bhejo jo aaj tension mein hain."

## Viral hone ke liye checklist

- Pehle 2 second mein hook bolo, intro ya "Namaste dosto" mat rakho.
- Subtitle zaroor lagao (Hindi mein), bahut log bina awaaz ke dekhte hain.
- Video 45 second se chhota rakho, taaki log poora dekhein.
- Ending ko shuruaat se jodo, taaki video loop ho kar dobara chale.
- Har Short par ek sawaal pucho aur pehla comment khud pin karo.
- Roz 1-2 Shorts daalo. Horror aur emotional dono ka mix chalao aur dekho kaunsa zyada chalta hai.
